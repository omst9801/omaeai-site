THE EXPERIENCE

One Kate.
So many ways to help.

A receptionist should do more than pick up the phone. Kate is being designed to give your callers a consistent, thoughtful experience and your team clear next steps.

01◉

She answers naturally

No endless “press 1” or “press 2” menus. Kate is designed to let people explain what they need in their own words.

02⌁

She learns your business

With your approved information, Kate can explain services, hours, and the next step for a caller.

03✳

She takes a helpful message

For requests she cannot complete, Kate can collect appropriate callback details for your team.

04↗

She knows when to hand off

When a caller needs a person, Kate can follow your business rules for a transfer or callback.

05≡

She keeps you informed

Planned call summaries help your team understand why someone called and what follow-up may be needed.

06◇

She works to your rules

You decide what Kate may say or do, which information she can use, and which requests need human approval.
